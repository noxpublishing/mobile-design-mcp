## Inspiration and reuse

Abstract ideas and methods are different from their concrete expression.
Study interaction principles, task structure, hierarchy and motion intent,
then create your own expression and use assets you own or are licensed to use.
Being inspired by an app is not automatically infringement; copying a screen,
its artwork, distinctive assets or a protected implementation can raise
different issues. Other rights, including trademarks and design rights, may
also apply depending on the jurisdiction and facts.

Neither attribution nor a “studies only” label supplies missing permission.
Noncommercial intent is not a universal copyright exception. Do not assume that
a Mobbin subscription, an award listing or public web access permits exporting,
republishing or sublicensing the content. Follow the relevant provider terms
and obtain required permissions before distributing reference captures.

The repository publishes no mirrored Mobbin screen library or downloaded
website bundles. Local imported captures and generated review packets stay
outside version control. Review public mockups and showcase assets separately
for permission, personal data and brand presentation before publication.

Independently created output does not become owned by this project merely
because the toolkit helped produce it. The public license still does not
permit recipients to use the covered toolkit for commercial development.
Neither the toolkit nor a generated packet certifies copyright clearance.
